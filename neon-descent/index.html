The loop

  • Walk into a red sector and it seals. Kill everything to open it.
  • Clear every sector on the floor to unlock the exit portal.
  • Glowing eyes and cores are weak points: hits there always crit.
  • Chain kills to raise your multiplier. Taking damage drains it.
  • Scrap you collect is kept when you die. Spend it in the Workshop.

Hostiles

  • SkitterFast melee crawler. Lunges after a short tell.
  • SentryWalks and fires three-round bursts.
  • WispFlying drone with a spread shot.
  • TickRuns at you and detonates. Shoot it near its friends.

Heavy hostiles

  • JuggernautSlams the floor. Jump the shockwave ring.
  • LancerSniper. The laser locks white a moment before it fires. Move.
  • The WardenEvery fifth floor. Plates shield its core.
  • ElitesGlowing aura, triple health, better loot.
