The loop

  • Walk into a red sector and it seals. Kill everything to open it.
  • Clear every sector on the floor to unlock the exit portal.
  • Press F or right-click to swing your blade. It knocks enemy shots out of the air.
  • Glowing eyes and cores are weak points: hits there always crit.
  • Chain kills to raise your multiplier. Taking damage drains it.
  • Scrap you collect is kept when you die. Spend it in the Workshop.
